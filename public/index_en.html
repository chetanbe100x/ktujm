About the University

To meet the challenges in the rapidly diversifying field of media and mass communication, the Government of Chhattisgarh took the momentous decision of establishing Kushabhau Thakre Patrakarita Avam Jansanchar Vishwavidyalaya (KTUJM), Raipur. Constituted through the State Legislature's Act No. 24 of 2004 and recognized under UGC Act 1956 section 2(f) and 12(B), KTUJM is mandated to fill the gap in media education through rigorous teaching, practical studio training, and advanced scholarly research in journalism, broadcasting, advertising, public relations, and allied digital frontiers.

Sprawled over a serene 50-acre green campus at Kathadih, Raipur, the university houses full-scale broadcast television studios, audio editing floors, Radio Pratidhwani 90.8 FM, cutting-edge computer labs, and a prestigious media library with over 25,000 reference volumes.

Programmes

Choose from specialized academic offerings structured under the National Education Policy (NEP 2020)

PG Diploma in Broadcast Journalism

Designed to impart rigorous studio newsroom anchoring, multi-camera television production, teleprompter delivery, and electronic news gathering.

PG Diploma in Photography

Provides in-depth creative and technical training in photojournalism, visual archiving, commercial portraiture, and digital lighting.

PG Diploma in Desktop Publishing

Practical training in pagination, Adobe InDesign, typography, newspaper layout design, infographics, and prepress publishing.

PG Diploma in Public Health & Media

Equips media practitioners with vital knowledge on health reporting, community advocacy, disease surveillance communication, and CSR.

B.A. in Mass Communication

Comprehensive undergraduate curriculum covering print journalism, radio broadcasting, digital storytelling, media ethics, and public relations.

B.A. in Electronic Media

Hands-on television production, audio mixing, news reporting, video editing, and live broadcast operations in university studios.

Bachelor of Business Admin (BBA)

Specialized management program focusing on media economics, brand communication, marketing principles, and media enterprise leadership.

M.A. in Mass Communication

Advanced research, communication theories, development journalism, policy studies, and media sociology.

M.A. in Electronic Media

Professional broadcast degree preparing graduates for national television networks, documentary filmmaking, and audio engineering.

M.A. in Advertising & PR

Corporate communications, digital advertising campaign analytics, crisis PR management, and consumer psychology.

Master of Journalism (M.J.)

Advanced investigative reporting, editorial writing, constitutional law, data journalism, and cyber jurisprudence.

Doctoral Programme

Ph.D. in Journalism & Mass Communication

The objective of the Ph.D. programme is to promote original empirical research, media policy critical discourse, and scholarly investigations aligned with UGC standards. Scholars explore emerging paradigms including algorithmic bias, AI in broadcast journalism, community radio empowerment, and regional media evolution.

Candidates are admitted through the National Level University Research Entrance Test (RET) or UGC-NET/JRF qualification followed by DRC viva-voce and mandatory 6-month pre-Ph.D. coursework.

Central Media Library

The central library of KTUJM is an intellectual repository housing over 25,000 volumes, rare archival newspapers, bound volumes of historic publications, and extensive subscriptions to national and international peer-reviewed journalism journals.

Equipped with INFLIBNET e-ShodhSindhu digital access, DELNET inter-library loans, automated barcode tracking, and an archival media museum, the library provides scholars and journalists an unparalleled academic sanctum.
